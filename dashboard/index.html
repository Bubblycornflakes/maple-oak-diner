Menu engineering: popularity against contribution margin

Each dish is placed by its share of orders inside its section of the QR menu (horizontal) and the margin it leaves after ingredients (vertical). The vertical rule is 70% of a fair share of the group's orders; the horizontal rule is the group's sales-weighted average margin. Stars sell well and earn well; plowhorses sell but earn little; puzzles earn but don't sell; dogs do neither. Dot size is orders per week.
